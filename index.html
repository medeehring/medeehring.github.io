About

I’m a PhD student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at Rice University, advised by Dr. Momona Yamagami. My research sits at the intersection of clinical data and engineering, using biosensors and machine learning to develop systems that enable rehabilitation at home without sacrificing professional oversight. I am currently developing an exergame platform for pediatric neurologic injury survivors with upper extremity motor impairments that affect their activities of daily living.

Before starting my PhD, I studied biomedical engineering at the University of Houston, and I worked as a biomedical engineering intern at Starling Medical. I care about research that translates into real benefits, which means building these systems alongside the children and disabled people who will actually use them.

Research Interests

  • Exergames and serious games for pediatric motor rehabilitation
  • Quality of movement assessment
  • Participatory and co-design methods in rehabilitation engineering
  • Machine learning for clinical and rehabilitation data
